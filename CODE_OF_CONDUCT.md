# Code of Conduct

Rise.js follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagree about code, not about people.
- No harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Assume good intent, and accept correction when you get something wrong.

These rules apply in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel run by the project.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, contact the maintainers privately through the contact details on the [organization profile](https://github.com/risejs). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts that break these rules.
